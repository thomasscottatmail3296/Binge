# TV Shows

Canonical location for television, streaming, web, animated, anime, miniseries, limited-series, and other episodic productions.

## Episode convention

    Show/
    └── Season 01/
        ├── S01E01 - Episode Title/
        │   └── S01E01 - Episode Title.md
        └── S01E02 - Episode Title/
            └── S01E02 - Episode Title.md

Supporting folders inside an episode directory are created only when real supporting material exists.
